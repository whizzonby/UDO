Udo Product Overview
1. What Udo is
Udo is a wedding planning and coordination platform designed to bring structure, clarity, and calm to the entire wedding journey.
At its core, Udo is not just a planning tool. It is a centralized system that helps couples manage their wedding, coordinate guests, communicate effectively, and experience their day with less stress.
The product is built around a simple idea:
Weddings are complex, but the experience of planning and living through them should feel calm, organized, and intentional.
Udo consolidates multiple fragmented workflows - planning, guest management, communication, logistics, gifting, and memories - into one cohesive experience.
2. What we want to achieve
The goal of Udo is to remove the invisible stress of wedding planning and coordination.
There are several key problems we are solving:
Couples currently juggle multiple tools (notes, spreadsheets, messaging apps, Pinterest, wedding websites, etc.) 
Guest communication is scattered and inconsistent 
Logistics (travel, accommodation, transport) are difficult to manage at scale 
There is no clear system for managing the wedding day itself 
The experience before and after the wedding is disconnected 
Udo aims to:
Centralize everything into one platform 
Provide clear structure without overwhelming the user 
Make guest coordination simple and intuitive 
Enable seamless communication before and during the event 
Support the wedding day with a calm, real-time layer 
Extend value beyond the wedding through memories and gratitude 
Ultimately, the product should make users feel:
In control 
Supported 
Less overwhelmed 
Able to enjoy the process 

3. Who we are targeting
Primary users (admin users)
Brides and grooms 
Couples planning their wedding 
Wedding planners 
Close collaborators (e.g., maid of honor, best man, family members helping with coordination) 
These users are responsible for organizing and managing the wedding.
Secondary users (guests)
Wedding guests receiving invitations 
Guests needing information (schedule, venue, travel, etc.) 
Guests contributing to the event (RSVP, photos, gifts) 
Guests are not expected to use the full app. Their experience is primarily through a simple, personalized link that allows them to access everything they need without friction.
4. How the product is structured
Udo is organized into a set of core areas, each with a clear role:
Home
A high-level overview that shows progress, key updates, and what needs attention.
Plan
The planning engine where users manage tasks, timelines, vendors, budgets, and seating.
Guests
The guest management system, including guest lists, invitations, communication, and guest experience setup.
Live
A real-time layer used in the final days and on the wedding day to provide updates, coordination, and reassurance.

Gallery
A space for inspiration and memories, including Pinterest-style ideas, photo uploads, and curated moments.
Registry
A simple gifting and contribution system that also supports post-wedding thank-you tracking.

5. The guest experience
Guests interact with Udo through a personalized link, not the full app.
Through this link, they can:
RSVP 
View event details and schedule 
Access venue and travel information 
Receive updates and announcements 
Upload photos 
View gallery content 
Access the registry 
This experience should be:
Clear 
Mobile-friendly 
Fast 
Easy to understand without instructions 
6. Key product principles
The product should be guided by the following principles:
Simplicity over complexity
Every feature should feel easy to use. Setup should be fast and intuitive.
Clarity over volume
Users should always know what matters, without being overwhelmed by too much information.
Structure without rigidity
The system should guide users but not feel restrictive.
Calm over urgency
The interface should reduce stress, not amplify it.
Consistency across the journey
The product should feel cohesive from planning through to the wedding day and beyond.
7. What makes Udo different
Udo differentiates itself by combining multiple functions into one integrated system:
Planning + guest management + communication + logistics + live coordination + memories 
A dynamic guest experience instead of a static wedding website 
A focus on emotional experience, not just task completion 
A product that continues to be useful after the wedding (gallery, archive, thank-you tracking) 
Most tools in this space solve one part of the problem. Udo aims to connect the entire journey.
8. Vision
The long-term vision for Udo is to become:
The default platform couples use to plan, manage, and experience their wedding from start to finish.
Over time, this could extend into:
deeper logistics support 
vendor integrations 
enhanced guest personalization 
expanded event types beyond weddings 
But the immediate focus is to deliver a high-quality, cohesive wedding experience that feels significantly better than existing solutions.
