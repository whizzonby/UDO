Redesign the entire Udo marketing landing page so it feels premium, calm, feminine, modern, editorial, soft, and highly intentional. The goal is to make a bride, couple, or planner immediately feel: “this understands my stress, this is elegant, and this solves a real problem.” The page must feel emotionally warm but commercially sharp. It should not feel generic SaaS, childish, fluffy, or overly romantic in a tacky way. It should feel like a luxury modern wedding product with clarity and structure.

Core brand direction

Udo means peace, and the landing page should embody that. The page should communicate that Udo helps people plan weddings in a calmer, more organized, more intelligent way. The tone should feel like premium guidance, not chaos management. Elegant but practical. Soft but not weak. Beautiful but not vague.

The page must answer, through design and copy, these questions without literally listing them:

Who is this for?
What does it do?
Why does it matter?
How does it work?
Why is it better than the messy alternative?

The landing page needs to feel clearer in value proposition than it currently does. Right now it looks nice, but it needs to convert better and explain the product more intelligently.

Typography direction

The current top-left Udo mark and font feel awkward and not premium enough. Improve this.

Use a more elevated typography pairing:

Headings: refined serif with editorial elegance, something in the spirit of Playfair Display / Cormorant Garamond / Canela-inspired / high-end modern serif
Body text: clean modern sans serif, something in the spirit of Inter / Satoshi / Neue Haas Grotesk / modern neutral UI font

Typography should clearly separate:

emotional premium brand voice in headings
crisp clarity in body copy

The wordmark “Udo” in the top left should feel more luxurious and deliberate. Refine spacing, size, alignment, and weight. The logo mark should feel softer and better integrated, not like a generic placeholder circle. Keep it simple, but more elegant.

General layout rules

The whole page needs better hierarchy and rhythm. Add more visual breathing room and stronger section pacing.

Use:

soft rounded cards with subtle shadows only if necessary
no over-decoration
no busy dividers
no random floating elements unless they are purposeful
large, clear sections that each communicate one thing well

The page should be clearly structured and scroll beautifully.
