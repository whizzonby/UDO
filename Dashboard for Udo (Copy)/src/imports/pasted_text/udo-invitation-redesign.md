# FIGMA PROMPT — UDO INVITATION EXPERIENCE OVERHAUL

Redesign the entire “Invitations” section of the Udo wedding planning app into a premium, emotionally elegant, modern invitation creation experience.

The current layout feels too dashboard-heavy and functional. The redesigned experience should feel editorial, luxurious, customizable, and emotionally immersive while still remaining extremely simple to use.

The inspiration should feel like:

* modern luxury wedding brands
* high-end Canva templates
* Apple-level simplicity
* Pinterest-worthy wedding aesthetics
* modern editorial magazine layouts
* soft romantic UI
* emotionally elevated but technologically clean

The experience should feel premium enough that users WANT to share and show off their invitation.

---

# OVERALL DESIGN DIRECTION

Use:

* white backgrounds
* large spacing
* soft shadows
* elegant card layouts
* subtle gradients
* minimal borders
* refined typography
* modern luxury wedding aesthetics

Accent colors:

* #FF3E9B
* #FF88BA
* #3A8B95
* #66D0BC

Use colors sparingly and intentionally.

The UI should NEVER feel overly pink or childish.

The experience should feel:
clean, modern, feminine, premium, calm, emotional.

---

# MAIN GOAL

Transform the invitation area from:
“basic invitation management”

into:

“a beautiful digital wedding invitation studio.”

---

# SECTION STRUCTURE

Redesign the invitation section into these flows:

1. Invitation Overview
2. Template Gallery
3. Invitation Editor
4. Send & Delivery
5. Guest Personalization
6. Invitation Analytics
7. Mobile Preview Experience
