Revise the Wedding Party section so it is no longer static. Every tab, filter, button, card, and chip must be clickable and must lead to a populated screen, modal, drawer, or changed state. No placeholder screens. No “ready for content” messages.

In the Wedding Party top-level sub-navigation, remove the Looks tab entirely. Merge Timeline and Wedding Day into one tab called Wedding Timeline because these are currently overlapping. Keep the useful tabs as: Overview, People, Responsibilities, Buzzes, Wedding Timeline, Travel, Rehearsal, Emergency. Remove Documents unless it has a clear practical use; if it remains, it must be renamed “Files & Speeches” and populated with useful wedding-party documents such as speeches, vows, fitting notes, emergency checklist, transport lists, and contact sheets.

The Responsibilities tab must become fully functional. Add view controls at the top: Kanban, Timeline, Priority, By Person, and List. These controls must visibly transform the layout when clicked. Kanban should show columns for Assigned, Acknowledged, In Progress, Needs Help, and Completed, with responsibility cards that can be dragged between columns. Timeline should show responsibilities arranged by date and time, with dependency markers and countdown labels. Priority should group responsibilities by Urgent, Soon, Normal, and Completed. By Person should group all responsibilities under each wedding party member, showing their workload, status, contact buttons, and outstanding items. List should show a clean sortable list with filters for role, person, status, date, and location.

Each responsibility card must be editable. When clicked, open a full detail drawer or modal with fields for title, description, assigned person, backup person, due date, due time, location, linked event, dependency, priority, status, notes, attachments, and communication history. Include buttons for Save Changes, Mark Complete, Send Buzz, Reassign, Delete, and Add Backup Person. Add a visible “+ Add Responsibility” button at the top and bottom of the Responsibilities tab. Clicking it must open a populated creation flow with templates such as Bride Morning Support, Ceremony Support, Transport Coordination, Speech Upload, Emergency Kit, Family Assistance, Rehearsal Support, and Reception Support.

The Wedding Timeline tab must combine the old Timeline and Wedding Day concepts into one powerful editable timeline. It should include pre-wedding, rehearsal, wedding morning, ceremony, cocktail hour, reception, after-party, and departure blocks. The bride/planner must be able to add new timeline items, edit existing items, delete items, drag-and-drop timeline items, reorder the day, and automatically adjust times when one item moves. For example, if Hair & Makeup is extended by 20 minutes, the following linked items should shift or show a conflict warning. Include an “Auto-adjust following times” toggle and a “Notify affected people” button. Each timeline item should show time, title, location, owner, linked responsibilities, affected people, notes, and status. Clicking any timeline item opens an edit drawer.

Populate the Travel tab fully. It should support destination weddings and local weddings without forcing unnecessary information. Include sections for travelling guests, hotel/villa assignments, room sharing, arrival times, airport transfers, shuttle schedules, car rental requests, carpool requests, pickup points, driver assignments, payment notes, and who has confirmed. Include a toggle for “Local wedding” versus “Destination wedding.” For local weddings, make travel optional and lightweight. For destination weddings, show deeper logistics such as villa assignments, amount owed, payment status, arrival airport, shuttle group, and assigned coordinator. Include shareable guest links so guests can submit travel details without downloading the app.

Populate the Rehearsal tab fully. Include rehearsal schedule, attendees, missing confirmations, rehearsal dinner details, roles to practise, processional order, speech practice, transport to rehearsal, and a checklist for what must be confirmed before the wedding day. Add buttons for Send Rehearsal Reminder, Edit Rehearsal Schedule, Assign Role, and Confirm Attendance. Every button must open a relevant populated modal.

Populate the Emergency tab fully. Include emergency contacts, planner contact, venue contact, driver contact, hair and makeup contact, photographer contact, backup vendor list, first aid notes, allergies, medication notes, dress emergency kit, weather backup plan, transport backup plan, missing person escalation, and one-tap emergency broadcast. Include calm language and a premium, non-alarming design. Add “Silent Emergency Mode” for discreetly alerting selected people.

The Buzzes tab must connect to Responsibilities, Travel, Rehearsal, and Wedding Timeline. A user should be able to send a Buzz from any responsibility or timeline item. Buzzes should support WhatsApp, SMS, email, and in-app delivery. Include message templates, tone selection, scheduled send, recipient groups, delivery status, read status, and response tracking.

The People tab must connect to everything. Each wedding party member profile should show responsibilities, timeline assignments, travel details, rehearsal status, emergency role, contact details, and buzz history. Their profile should be editable and should allow direct communication.

The Overview tab should update based on all other tabs. If a responsibility is overdue, the readiness score changes. If travel is incomplete, travel readiness changes. If rehearsal is not confirmed, rehearsal readiness changes. If emergency contacts are missing, emergency readiness changes. Overview must show smart operational alerts that link directly to the relevant tab and item.

Strict interaction requirement: every visible button, tab, chip, card, filter, status badge, icon, and link must do something. It must either change the visible layout, open a populated modal, open a populated drawer, navigate to a populated detail page, update status, or trigger a visible confirmation. Do not create decorative buttons. Do not create static fake controls.

The final result should feel like a real wedding-party coordination operating system: elegant, simple, emotional, functional, and realistic enough for a bride, planner, or maid of honor to use during the months before the wedding and on the wedding day itself.
