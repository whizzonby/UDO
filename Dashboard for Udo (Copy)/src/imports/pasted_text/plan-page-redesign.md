Redesign the UDO app Plan page completely. Keep the same overall brand world as the updated UDO home page, but make this page feel cleaner, more useful, less cluttered, less repetitive, and far more direct.

This is a mobile-first wedding planning app for women and couples who want to feel calm, organized, emotionally supported, and stylish while planning. The Plan page should feel like a beautiful command center, not a list of long cards and not a stack of endless modals. It should feel like a modern, polished, calming planning product that is ready to go live.

CORE GOAL

The new Plan page must:

reduce overwhelm
remove unnecessary clicks
show everything important in a structured way
let the user understand where they are, what matters now, and what to do next
connect directly to onboarding choices and display them in a meaningful way
feel emotionally intelligent, warm, grown, gentle, and premium
look visually aligned with the new UDO home page

Do not make this page feel technical, busy, clinical, corporate, or overly dense.
